# eFrontier CAD Design — Business Plan

## Market Overview
Frontier hardware rarely sells on volume; it sells on being the only platform that closes a research loop. EmbeddedOS targets the control and instrumentation layer, where a general-purpose embedded stack replaces the bespoke FPGA firmware most laboratories otherwise maintain themselves.

## Target Segments

| Segment | TAM 2030 | EOS Target Share |
|---|---|---|
| Quantum control electronics | $4.2B | 1.5% |
| Integrated photonics instrumentation | $8B | 0.8% |
| Neuromorphic compute | $3B | 1.2% |
| Space robotics | $6B | 0.9% |

## Revenue Model

- Instrument licensing to research institutions and national laboratories
- eOS real-time control stack licensing for quantum and photonic platforms
- Co-development contracts with academic and government programmes
- Long-term support agreements tied to multi-year research grants
