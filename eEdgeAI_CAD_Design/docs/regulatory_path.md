## Key Standards

- **ISO 26262**: Functional safety for automotive sensor fusion products
- **IEC 62471**: Photobiological safety of lamps and lamp systems
- **EN 301 489-1**: EMC requirements for radio equipment
- **IEC 62368-1**: Audio/video and information technology equipment safety
