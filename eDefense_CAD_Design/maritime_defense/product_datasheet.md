# Maritime Defence Systems — Product Datasheet
> **Revision:** v1.0 | **Date:** 2026-08-08 | **Status:** Design Phase

## Product Overview
Coastal and harbour surveillance node fusing X-band radar returns, AIS reception, and electro-optical tracking to detect small craft that AIS alone would miss. Deployed on fixed towers and offshore platforms, feeding a maritime domain awareness picture over redundant backhaul.

## Electrical Specifications — eMDS-800
| Parameter | Specification |
|---|---|
| **Processor** | TI AM6254 quad Cortex-A53 |
| **Radar** | IWR6843AOP 60GHz mmWave, 300m small-craft detection |
| **AIS** | Dual-channel 161.975/162.025MHz Class-A reception |
| **Electro-optical** | Dual sensor: 4K visible and LWIR thermal |
| **Detection range** | 12 nmi vessel, 3 nmi RHIB-class target |
| **Backhaul** | 10GbE fibre primary, LTE Cat-4 backup |
| **Environment** | IP67, -25degC to +60degC, salt fog qualified |
