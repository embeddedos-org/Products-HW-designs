# Humanitarian Field Systems — Product Datasheet
> **Revision:** v1.0 | **Date:** 2026-08-08 | **Status:** Design Phase

## Product Overview
Field deployment kit providing connectivity, beneficiary registration and cold chain monitoring for humanitarian operations in areas without infrastructure. Solar-powered with a repairable design, since a device that cannot be fixed with locally available parts becomes waste at the first fault.

## Electrical Specifications — eHUM-400
| Parameter | Specification |
|---|---|
| **Processor** | TI AM6254 quad Cortex-A53 |
| **Connectivity** | Satellite backhaul, Wi-Fi 6 local network, LoRa mesh |
| **Registration** | Offline-capable beneficiary registration with sync |
| **Cold chain** | 8 monitored vaccine and medicine storage channels |
| **Power** | 40W solar with 5-day autonomy, 12V vehicle input |
| **Repairability** | Socketed modules, no adhesives, documented spares |
| **Environment** | IP66, -10degC to +55degC, dust and sand resistant |

## PCB Specifications
