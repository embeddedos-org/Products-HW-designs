# eMedical CAD Design — Business Plan

## Market Overview
Global medical device market projected at $800B by 2030. EmbeddedOS targets the embedded electronics layer across diagnostic, surgical, and patient care segments.

## Revenue Model
- Hardware platform licensing for IEC 60601-1 certified designs
- eOS Medical stack licensing (royalty per device)
- Cloud connectivity and remote monitoring subscriptions
- Regulatory consulting and certification support services
