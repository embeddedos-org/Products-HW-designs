# eAerospace — Regulatory Path

## Certifications Required

| Product | Standard | Authority | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| Avionics hardware | DO-254 Level A | FAA / EASA | 36 months |
| Avionics software | DO-178C Level A | FAA / EASA | 36 months |
| UAV flight controller | DO-178C Level C | FAA Part 107 | 18 months |
| Space OBC | ECSS-E-ST-50-12C | ESA / NASA | 24 months |
| Aircraft components | AS9100D | FAA / EASA | 12 months |

## Key Standards
- **DO-254**: Design Assurance Guidance for Airborne Electronic Hardware
- **DO-178C**: Software Considerations in Airborne Systems
- **MIL-STD-461**: EMC requirements for military equipment
- **ECSS-E-ST-50-12C**: SpaceWire links, nodes, routers and networks
