# eEdgeAI CAD Design — Business Plan

## Market Overview
Edge inference is moving off general-purpose CPUs and onto dedicated NPUs as latency, privacy and connectivity constraints make cloud round-trips untenable for real-time control. EmbeddedOS targets the board and runtime layer beneath the model, where the hardware/software contract is set.

## Target Segments

| Segment | TAM 2030 | EOS Target Share |
|---|---|---|
| Edge AI accelerators | $28B | 0.6% |
| Embedded vision | $21B | 0.9% |
| Voice and audio front ends | $9B | 0.7% |
| Robotics perception and SLAM | $14B | 1.1% |

## Revenue Model

- Hardware licensing of eOS-certified inference modules
- Runtime and model-compiler licensing for the eAI toolchain
- Per-device royalty on the eOS NPU hardware abstraction layer
- Model optimisation and quantisation services
