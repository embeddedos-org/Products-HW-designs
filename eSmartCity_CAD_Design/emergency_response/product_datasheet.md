# Emergency Response Systems — Product Datasheet
> **Revision:** v1.0 | **Date:** 2026-08-08 | **Status:** Design Phase

## Product Overview
Deployable incident command unit providing dispatch, responder tracking and multi-agency interoperability at the scene. Designed to establish a local command network in under five minutes without relying on surviving fixed infrastructure.

## Electrical Specifications — eEMR-800
| Parameter | Specification |
|---|---|
| **Processor** | TI AM6254 quad Cortex-A53 |
| **Deployment time** | <5 minutes from case-open to operational network |
| **Local network** | Wi-Fi 6 access point plus sub-GHz responder mesh |
| **Backhaul** | 5G, satellite, or wired, with automatic failover |
| **Responder tracking** | GNSS plus UWB for indoor positioning |
| **Capacity** | 250 tracked responders, 60 concurrent voice channels |
| **Endurance** | 18 hours on internal battery, hot-swappable |
