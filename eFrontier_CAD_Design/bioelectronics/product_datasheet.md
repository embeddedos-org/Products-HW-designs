# Bioelectronics Research Platforms — Product Datasheet
> **Revision:** v1.0 | **Date:** 2026-08-08 | **Status:** Research

## Product Overview
Multi-channel electrophysiology platform for neural interface research, recording 256 channels at 30 kSPS while delivering charge-balanced stimulation on any channel. Stimulation artefact recovery is the design driver, since a recording amplifier that saturates for 10 ms loses exactly the response the experiment is measuring.

## Electrical Specifications — eBIO-500
| Parameter | Specification |
|---|---|
| **Recording channels** | 256, 30 kSPS per channel, 16-bit |
| **Input noise** | 2.1 uVrms, 1 Hz to 10 kHz |
| **Stimulation** | Charge-balanced biphasic, +/-5 mA, 8 independent channels |
| **Artefact recovery** | <400 us to within 1% of baseline |
| **Impedance measurement** | Per-channel, 1 kHz, 10 kOhm to 10 MOhm |
| **Isolation** | 5 kVrms patient isolation barrier |
| **Biocompatibility** | ISO 10993 assessed materials in the wetted path |
