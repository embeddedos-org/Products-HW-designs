# eFrontier CAD Design — Regulatory Path

## Certifications Required

| Product | Standard | Authority | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quantum control electronics | CE / EN 61010-1 | Notified body | 9 months |
| Photonics instrumentation | IEC 60825-1 laser safety | Notified body | 12 months |
| Bioelectronics research platform | ISO 10993 biocompatibility | Notified body | 18 months |
| Space robotics controller | ECSS-Q-ST-60C | ESA | 24 months |

## Key Standards

- **IEC 60825-1**: Safety of laser products, applies to all photonic designs
- **EN 61010-1**: Safety requirements for laboratory measurement equipment
- **ISO 10993**: Biological evaluation of medical and bioelectronic devices
- **ECSS-Q-ST-60C**: Space product assurance for EEE components
