# eAerospace CAD Design — Business Plan

## Market Overview
The global aerospace electronics market is projected to reach $95B by 2030, driven by UAV proliferation, commercial space expansion, and avionics modernization. EmbeddedOS targets the embedded systems layer across all segments.

## Target Markets
| Segment | TAM 2030 | EOS Target Share |
|---|---|---|
| Commercial avionics | $28B | 0.5% |
| UAV/drone systems | $45B | 1.0% |
| Space systems | $22B | 0.3% |

## Revenue Model
- Hardware licensing of eOS-certified flight computers
- Royalty on DO-178C/DO-254 certified software stack
- Simulation and digital twin services via EoSim
- Maintenance and OTA update subscriptions
